Privacy Policy & Non-Prediction Disclaimer

Explicit Non-Prediction Disclaimer

AI Palmistry does NOT claim to scientifically predict your future, health, longevity, financial wealth, career trajectory, or personality traits. Palmistry is an ancient cultural art, not an empirical science. All interpretations generated by this software are for entertainment, personal curiosity, and self-reflection only.

1. No Medical or Financial Advice

Under no circumstances should any result from this application be considered medical, psychological, financial, legal, or professional advice. Always consult certified medical professionals or accredited financial advisors for health and financial matters.

2. Image Handling & Data Privacy

Uploaded palm images are processed locally by our backend computer vision engine for feature extraction. We do not sell your personal imagery or biomechanics. Intermediate debug images are stored securely on the server system.

3. Distinction Between AI Detection and Tradition

Our platform cleanly isolates algorithmic computer vision detection (line lengths, pixel coordinates, curvature scores) from traditional palmistry interpretations (symbolic meaning).
