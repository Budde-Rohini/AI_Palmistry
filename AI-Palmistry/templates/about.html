About AI Palmistry

AI Palmistry bridges computer vision technology with centuries of traditional palmistry lore to provide a non-predictive, self-reflective exploration of hand feature geometry.

How Computer Vision Works

Our backend uses Google MediaPipe Hand Landmarker to identify 21 key coordinate points across the hand structure. OpenCV filters then preprocess the palm area with contrast normalization (CLAHE), bilateral smoothing, and adaptive thresholding to detect major creases (Heart, Head, Life, and Fate lines).

Traditional Lore Integration

Measurements extracted by computer vision are evaluated against a structured rule matrix derived from traditional palmistry references. Crucially, these interpretations are explicitly presented as symbolic traditions rather than deterministic predictions.
